# Kill percents

Generated by `KILLTABLE=1 KILLTABLE_OUT=docs/kill-percents.md npx vitest run tests/killpercent.test.ts` (probe: `tests/tools/killProbe.ts`).

Lowest damage percent (steps of 5) at which the move KOs the victim on Skyline Classic. Moves are fresh and uncharged; the victim gets no DI and no hitstun cancel. A KO only counts if it lands before the victim is safe again (on solid ground or a ledge), so a victim that idles off the edge never counts. Three figures per cell:

- **recover**: a HARD CPU recovers (drifting home, jumping at once, no off-stage attacks) the moment hitstun ends.
- **best**: the best recovery found: every scripted mix of double-jump timing, side / neutral special, up-special timing and aim (`RecoveryPolicy`) is tried and the KO only counts if all of them fail. This is what a victim who recovers well sees, and the figure the design target is judged on.
- **strict** (brackets): a KO within 15 frames of hitstun ending (pure launch distance, no recovery at all).
- **Cell**: `recover` + blast zone (T top, S side, B bottom = launched too far out to make it back) / `best` + zone (`strict`); a single figure when all three agree. `-` = never up to 300%.
- **Victims**: light gizmo (weight 88), mid nova (100), heavy brick (120). **Centre** = middle of the main platform; **ledge** = 16 px from the right edge, launched outward (backward moves are done with the attacker's back to the ledge).
- **Moves**: the three smashes, the two aerials with the most knockback (spikes excluded), the strongest throw and the ultimate (performed for real with full meter). For multi-hit moves the hardest launcher is used and earlier hit groups add their damage once.
- **Design target** (`KILL_TARGETS`, `KILL_TARGETS=1 npx vitest run tests/killpercent.test.ts`): strongest move kills a middleweight from centre at 105-130%, typical kill moves at 120-150% (best-recovery rule).
