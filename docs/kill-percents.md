# Kill percents

Generated by `KILLTABLE=1 npx vitest run tests/killpercent.test.ts` (probe: `tests/tools/killProbe.ts`).

Lowest damage percent (steps of 5) at which the move KOs the victim on Skyline Classic. Moves are fresh and uncharged; the victim gets no DI and no hitstun cancel, then a HARD CPU recovers (drifting home and jumping at once) the moment hitstun ends. A KO only counts if it lands before the victim is safe again (on solid ground or a ledge), so a victim that idles off the edge never counts.

- **Cell**: `recover` percent + blast zone (T top, S side, B bottom = launched too far out to make it back). In brackets: the `strict` percent, a KO within 15 frames of hitstun ending (pure launch distance), when it differs. `-` = never up to 300%.
- **Victims**: light rime (weight 80), mid nova (100), heavy brick (108). **Centre** = middle of the main platform; **ledge** = 16 px from the right edge, launched outward (backward moves are done with the attacker's back to the ledge).
- **Moves**: the three smashes, the two aerials with the most knockback (spikes excluded), the strongest throw and the ultimate (performed for real with full meter). For multi-hit moves the hardest launcher is used and earlier hit groups add their damage once.
- **Design target** (`KILL_TARGETS`, `KILL_TARGETS=1 npx vitest run tests/killpercent.test.ts`): strongest move kills a middleweight from centre at 95-125%, typical kill moves at 120-155%.
